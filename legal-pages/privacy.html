Privacy Policy

Your privacy on VibeVerse

This policy explains the personal information VibeVerse may process when you visit, sign in, publish content or use community tools.

Effective date: 17 November 2026. This public policy reflects the current platform design; specific features may be unavailable during development.

1. Information you give us

When you register or use account features, VibeVerse may process your account email, username, profile name, profile photo, authentication information and profile settings. Public content you choose to publish, including videos, photos, captions, comments and interactions, may be visible to others depending on your chosen settings and feature availability. Do not publish information that you want to keep private.

2. Information generated when you use the service

The platform may process content views, likes, follows, engagement events, reports, upload and streaming metadata, session/security events and approximate technical information required for functionality and abuse prevention. Some preferences and temporary drafts may be stored locally on your device.

3. How information is used

  • To provide accounts, feeds, uploads and community features.
  • To protect accounts, investigate reports and reduce harmful activity.
  • To maintain, debug and improve the reliability of the service.
  • To support approved advertising and reporting where applicable.

4. Service providers

VibeVerse uses hosted infrastructure and may use third-party providers such as Supabase for authentication/data services, Vercel for web hosting, and other media infrastructure for supported features. These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services. Features and vendors can change as the platform develops.

5. Google advertising and cookies

When advertising is approved and enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalize advertisements. Depending on your location, applicable choices and consent requirements may apply. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner-sites information page and review your Google ad preferences. Showing an AdSense script does not mean ads are currently active.

6. Visibility and sharing

Public profiles, videos, photos and interactions may be viewed and shared by other users. Information may also be disclosed to service providers for platform operations or where required by applicable law and valid legal process. Avoid uploading sensitive personal information in public posts.

7. Storage and retention

Account information and published content may remain stored for as long as needed to provide the service, fulfill legal or safety obligations, address disputes, or maintain relevant records. Retention varies by data type and the platform's evolving features. A local draft deleted from your device may not be the same as deleting information stored on the server.

8. Your choices

You can manage available profile, account and content settings in VibeVerse. If you need help with access, correction, deletion or a privacy concern, see our Contact & Support page for currently available channels. We will not describe a feature as self-service unless it is actually available in the app.

9. Young users

Do not share personal addresses, school details, financial credentials or other sensitive information publicly. Safety-related reporting may be available in supported app areas. Features involving payments, earnings or contracts may have additional age requirements and must be managed by an eligible adult where applicable.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this policy as services and legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when this happens.
