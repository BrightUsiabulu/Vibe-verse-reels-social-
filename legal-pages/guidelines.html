Community Guidelines

Make every vibe a safer one.

VibeVerse is for creative expression and community. These expectations apply to videos, photos, comments, live sessions where available, and account behavior.

Be respectful

Do not bully, stalk, harass, threaten, dox or target others with hateful abuse. Respect consent and personal boundaries.

Protect young people

Do not expose children or teens to exploitative or inappropriate material, seek their sensitive information, or post content that endangers them. Do not share someone's location or private identifying details without permission.

Keep content lawful and authentic

Do not promote fraud, scams, dangerous acts, violent threats or illegal activity. Avoid deceptive impersonation, misleading claims and coordinated manipulation.

Respect creative ownership

Upload videos, photographs, music and clips only when you own them or have the appropriate permissions. Respect requests involving privacy and personal likeness.

Don't spam

Avoid fake accounts, automated engagement, repetitive promotions, unsolicited messages or links designed to mislead users.

Report problems responsibly

Where a video, profile, comment or other supported item offers a Report action, use it to flag the content for review. You can also find account help and reporting information at Contact & Support. Do not disclose sensitive personal information in public comments.

Our approach

We may restrict reported content or accounts where appropriate and technically supported. Some moderation features are still under development; we cannot promise immediate review or removal. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services instead of relying on in-app reporting.
