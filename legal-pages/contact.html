Contact & Support

Getting help with VibeVerse

Find the right place for account questions, reports and privacy requests. We won't ask you to publish sensitive information on a public page.

Account and sign-in help

Open the VibeVerse Account Center for available sign-in, recovery and account management options. Never share a password or one-time verification code with anyone.

Report harmful or unauthorized content

If an item in VibeVerse provides a Report option, use it to report harmful content, impersonation, suspected scams or violations of our Community Guidelines. Reports are subject to the moderation systems available for that feature. Do not include passwords or payment credentials.

Privacy and account data

Use the profile and account controls available in the VibeVerse app to review or change your information. Consult our Privacy Policy for categories of data handled by the platform. A dedicated external support email and privacy-request form have not yet been published; this page will be updated when a verified support channel is operational.

Technical problems

If a feature is unavailable, try refreshing the app or signing in again. Never send authentication credentials, banking details or private identity documents through public comments.

Emergency situations

For an urgent safety issue, contact appropriate local emergency services. VibeVerse is not an emergency-response service.
